# TREE HEY Creative Direction

## Concept

The site is a living field notebook: part personal exhibition, part collection of
observations. It should reveal curiosity and taste instead of presenting a
traditional resume.

The selected visual target is `docs/design-target-living-field-notebook.png`.

## Structure

1. Poster
2. Fragments
3. Experiments
4. Lens
5. Playground
6. Guestbook
7. Index
8. Last Page

## Visual System

- Warm paper: `#f1eee5`
- Near-black ink: `#111210`
- Moss: `#59633a`
- Faded sky: `#9eb9c5`
- Tomato: `#e75638`
- Display type: Syne
- Reading type: Space Grotesk

Paper grain, torn edges, tape, pins, contact sheets, compact captions, and
handwritten annotations form one shared material language. Layouts use
asymmetry, overlap, varied scale, and generous quiet space.

## Motion

- Poster letterforms separate gently as the page begins to scroll.
- Collage pieces reveal with staggered position and slight rotation.
- Project images use pinned, layered transitions rather than identical cards.
- Lens contact sheets move horizontally.
- Annotations draw or slide into place.
- Motion is tactile and editorial, never neon, cyber, or effects-first.

## Constraints

- Keep the first viewport close to the selected visual target.
- Show a hint of the next chapter at each major transition.
- Do not use glassmorphism, gradient orbs, HUD motifs, terminal styling, nested
  cards, repeated pill controls, or a resume-style skills section.
- Preserve real project screenshots and personal photography.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all content usable on mobile.
